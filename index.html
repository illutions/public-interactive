ASSET LICENSES
    JET ENGINE
    A turbofan engine produces thrust by accelerating air through its core and bypass.
    Fan
    The fan draws in air and directs it into the engine core and bypass.
    Compressor
    The compressor raises the pressure of the air before combustion.
    Shaft
    The shaft transfers rotational energy from the turbine to the compressor and fan.
    Combustion Chamber
    The combustion chamber mixes compressed air with fuel and ignites it.
    Turbine
    The turbine extracts energy from the hot gases to drive the shaft.
    Nozzle
    The nozzle accelerates the exhaust flow and helps generate thrust.
    Inner Fixed Structure
    The inner fixed structure supports internal components and guides airflow through the engine.
    Nacelle
    The nacelle surrounds the engine and guides airflow around it.
